# AING-CF: Core Framework
The AING-CF module is the foundation of the entire AING™ ecosystem. It establishes the essential principles, lifecycle, roles, and layers that are common to all other specialized modules. Mastery of the Core Framework is the first step for any individual or organization looking to adopt AING™.
## Overview
AING-CF provides the universal language and operating model for human-AI collaboration. It is not domain-specific but rather a meta-framework that can be applied to any type of work, from software development to marketing campaigns to scientific research. It introduces the fundamental concepts of Co-Intelligence, Orchestration, and continuous, data-driven Evolution.
## Key Concepts
### The Five Principles
The Core Framework is built on the five guiding principles: Co-Intelligence, Orchestration, Transparency, Learning, and Rhythm. These principles form the philosophical backbone of AING™ and ensure its application remains effective and ethical.
### The Adaptive Lifecycle
AING-CF defines the five-phase lifecycle (Initialize → Orchestrate → Co-Create → Validate → Evolve) that provides a structured yet flexible path for any initiative. It emphasizes fluidity and real-time adaptation over rigid, gated processes.
### Human + AI Roles
The module introduces the new collaborative roles of Strategist, Creator, and Guardian for humans, and Orchestrator, Co-pilot, and Analyst for AI agents. This redefines the team structure around the concept of a co-intelligent unit.
## Tools & Templates
### The AING™ Canvas
The primary tool introduced in AING-CF is the AING™ Canvas. This strategic template is used in the Initialize phase to ensure every project starts with a clear, outcome-oriented focus. It captures the "why" before the "how" and serves as the primary input for the AI Orchestrator.
### The Flowboard
The concept of the Flowboard is central to the Core Framework. It represents the shift from static plans to a living, AI-managed visualization of the workflow, providing unprecedented transparency and real-time control.
## Certification Path
Understanding the AING-CF module is a prerequisite for all AING™ certifications. It forms the entire basis for the **AING™ Foundation Certificate (AFC)** and is a significant component of the **AING™ Practitioner Certificate (APC)**.
## Use Cases
- **Organizational Transformation:** A company uses AING-CF to establish a new, unified way of working across all departments.
- **Project Kick-offs:** A project team uses the AING™ Canvas to ensure clear alignment and strategic focus before beginning a new initiative.
- **Establishing a PMO Alternative:** An organization replaces its traditional, process-heavy Project Management Office (PMO) with a lightweight team of AING™ Strategists and Guardians who oversee an AI Orchestrator.
