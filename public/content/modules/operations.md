# AING-OM: Operations Management
The AING-OM module focuses on applying the AING™ framework to the domains of IT Service Management (ITSM), DevOps, and Site Reliability Engineering (SRE). It provides a model for creating intelligent, self-healing, and highly automated operational environments.
## Overview
Traditional operations management is often reactive, responding to incidents and requests as they occur. AING-OM shifts the paradigm to a predictive and proactive model. By leveraging AI to analyze operational data, the framework can anticipate issues before they impact users and automate complex resolution procedures.
## Key Concepts
### From ITSM and DevOps to AI-Ops
AING-OM integrates the principles of ITSM (like incident and change management) and the culture of DevOps with the power of AI. The result is an AI-Ops environment where an AI Orchestrator manages the flow of changes, deployments, and incident responses.
### Intelligent Change Management
Instead of a manual Change Advisory Board (CAB), AING-OM uses an AI-powered change management system. The AI agent analyzes a proposed change, assesses its potential risk by cross-referencing it with the system's architecture and historical incident data, and can automatically approve low-risk changes. High-risk changes are automatically flagged for a "Collaboration Moment" with human experts.
### Predictive Monitoring and Self-Healing
The AI Analyst in the AING-OM context continuously monitors system telemetry (logs, metrics, traces). It uses machine learning to learn the normal operating parameters of the system and can predict potential failures before they happen. For common issues, it can trigger automated remediation workflows, creating a self-healing system.
## Tools & Templates
### Operational Resilience Canvas
A tool for mapping out the critical services of an organization, defining their Service Level Objectives (SLOs), and designing the AI-driven monitoring and response strategies. This canvas is used to configure the AI Analyst and Orchestrator for the operational environment.
### Incident Swarm Room
A virtual collaboration space automatically created by the AI Orchestrator when a critical incident occurs. The AI invites the necessary human experts, provides them with a complete summary of the incident data and its analysis, and suggests potential root causes, dramatically reducing the Mean Time to Resolution (MTTR).
## Certification Path
The AING-OM module is a specialized track within the **AING™ Practitioner Certificate (APC)** and is highly relevant for professionals seeking the **AING™ Architect Certificate (AAC)**. It is ideal for SREs, DevOps engineers, and IT operations managers.
## Use Cases
- **Zero-Downtime E-commerce Platform:** A large online retailer uses AING-OM to predict traffic spikes and automatically scale infrastructure, while self-healing systems handle common issues without human intervention.
- **Global SaaS Provider:** A software-as-a-service company implements Intelligent Change Management to safely deploy hundreds of code changes per day across a complex microservices architecture.
- **Financial Services IT:** A bank uses AING-OM to ensure the stability and resilience of its critical trading platforms, using predictive monitoring to prevent costly outages.
