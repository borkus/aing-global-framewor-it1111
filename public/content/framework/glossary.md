# AING™ Glossary of Terms
A comprehensive glossary of key terms and concepts used within the Artificial Intelligence Next Generation (AING™) Framework.
---
### A
**AI Analyst**
: An AI agent role dedicated to the continuous collection, analysis, and reporting of data for the Validate and Governance layers.
**AI Co-pilot**
: A specialized AI agent that works directly alongside a human Creator to augment their skills and automate tasks within a specific domain (e.g., coding, design).
**AI Orchestrator**
: The master AI agent that manages the entire workflow, including planning, resource allocation, and real-time adaptation. It is the core of the Execution Layer.
### C
**Co-Creation**
: The third phase of the AING™ lifecycle, where human-AI teams collaboratively execute tasks to produce work products.
**Co-Intelligence**
: A core principle of AING™, stating that the synergy of human and machine intelligence is the primary driver of value.
**Collaboration Moment**
: A scheduled, data-rich interaction where human teams review AI-generated insights to make strategic decisions. This replaces traditional Agile ceremonies.
**Creator**
: A human role in AING™ for a domain expert who works with AI Co-pilots to produce tangible work.
### E
**Evolution Layer**
: One of the four operating layers of AING™, focused on learning and continuous improvement of the entire system.
**Evolve (Phase)**
: The fifth and final phase of the AING™ lifecycle, where learnings are integrated to refine the product, process, and AI models.
**Execution Layer**
: One of the four operating layers of AING™, where the work of co-creation happens. It is managed by the AI Orchestrator.
### F
**Flowboard**
: A dynamic, real-time visualization of the workflow, managed by the AI Orchestrator. It is the primary interface for the Execution Layer.
### G
**Guardian**
: A human role in AING™ responsible for ethical oversight, quality assurance, and acting as the final human-in-the-loop for critical decisions.
**Governance Layer**
: One of the four operating layers of AING™, providing continuous, AI-augmented oversight for quality, compliance, and ethics.
### I
**Initialize**
: The first phase of the AING™ lifecycle, focused on defining the desired outcome and assembling the human-AI team.
**Insight Loop**
: A conceptual tool representing the systematic process of capturing, analyzing, and acting on data to drive evolution.
### O
**Orchestrate (Phase)**
: The second phase of the AING™ lifecycle, where the AI Orchestrator designs and activates the dynamic workflow.
**Orchestration**
: A core principle of AING™, which posits that workflows should be fluidly coordinated by intelligent systems rather than rigid processes.
### S
**Strategy Layer**
: The highest of the four operating layers of AING™, concerned with defining the purpose, vision, and desired outcomes.
**Strategist**
: A human role in AING™ responsible for setting the direction, defining outcomes, and making high-level strategic decisions.
### V
**Validate**
: The fourth phase of the AING™ lifecycle, where progress and quality are continuously measured against the defined outcomes using AI-driven analytics.
