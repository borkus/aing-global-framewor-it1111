# Roles in AING™
AING™ introduces a new paradigm of collaboration that redefines traditional roles. Instead of fixed job titles, AING™ focuses on dynamic roles that describe the nature of the interaction between humans and AI within the framework.
## Human Roles
Human roles in AING™ are elevated to focus on strategy, creativity, and oversight—areas where human intellect excels.
### 1. The Strategist
The Strategist is responsible for setting the direction and defining the desired outcomes. They are the "why" behind the work. They interface with AI to understand the problem space but ultimately use their judgment and experience to make the final strategic decisions.
- **Key Responsibilities:**
  - Defining business objectives and success metrics.
  - Framing problems for AI analysis.
  - Making final decisions on priorities and direction.
  - Ensuring ethical considerations are addressed.
### 2. The Creator
The Creator is the domain expert, the innovator, and the problem-solver. They work alongside AI Co-pilots to produce the actual work, whether it's code, design, content, or strategy. They leverage AI as a powerful tool to augment their skills, not replace them.
- **Key Responsibilities:**
  - Generating novel ideas and solutions.
  - Using AI tools to accelerate development and creation.
  - Applying deep domain expertise to complex problems.
  - Reviewing and refining AI-generated work.
### 3. The Guardian
The Guardian is the ethical and quality steward of the system. They are responsible for ensuring that the work aligns with organizational values, quality standards, and ethical principles. They act as the ultimate human-in-the-loop for critical decisions.
- **Key Responsibilities:**
  - Overseeing the ethical application of AI.
  - Validating the quality and integrity of the final output.
  - Intervening when AI-driven processes deviate from desired norms.
  - Championing human-centric design and outcomes.
## AI Agent Roles
AI agents in AING™ are not just tools; they are active participants in the workflow, each with a specialized role.
### 1. The Orchestrator
The Orchestrator is the central nervous system of the AING™ framework. This master AI agent is responsible for managing the entire workflow, from planning and resource allocation to monitoring and adaptation.
- **Key Functions:**
  - Dynamically generating and adjusting project plans.
  - Assigning tasks to the most appropriate human or AI agent.
  - Monitoring progress and predicting bottlenecks.
  - Facilitating communication between all team members.
### 2. The Co-pilot
Co-pilots are specialized AI agents that work directly with human Creators. They act as intelligent assistants, augmenting the Creator's skills and automating routine tasks. There can be many different types of Co-pilots (e.g., a Coding Co-pilot, a Design Co-pilot, a Research Co-pilot).
- **Key Functions:**
  - Generating code, text, or design drafts.
  - Performing data analysis and research.
  - Automating repetitive and time-consuming tasks.
  - Providing real-time suggestions and feedback.
### 3. The Analyst
The Analyst is an AI agent dedicated to the Validate phase. It continuously collects and analyzes data from the project, providing unbiased, real-time insights into performance, quality, and progress toward the defined outcomes.
- **Key Functions:**
  - Tracking KPIs and success metrics.
  - Analyzing user feedback and behavior.
  - Running automated tests and quality checks.
  - Generating performance dashboards and reports.
