# Capability Module: Robotic Process Automation (RPA)
## 1. Principles
Robotic Process Automation (RPA) in the AING™ framework is based on the principle of "intelligent automation." This means moving beyond simple, brittle scripts to create resilient, AI-enhanced bots that can handle exceptions and adapt to changes in user interfaces and processes. The goal is to free humans from high-volume, repetitive digital tasks to focus on value-added work.
## 2. Practices
AING™ promotes a centralized "Center of Excellence" approach to RPA. This includes systematically identifying and prioritizing automation opportunities (Initialize), using a low-code platform for rapid bot development (Orchestrate), involving business users in the design and testing of bots (Co-Create), continuously monitoring bot performance and ROI (Validate), and using AI to suggest new processes for automation (Evolve).
## 3. Roles
- **RPA Developer (Creator):** Designs, develops, and maintains the software bots.
- **Business Analyst (Strategist):** Works with business units to identify and document processes suitable for automation.
- **RPA Controller (Guardian):** Manages the fleet of bots, scheduling their execution and handling exceptions.
- **AI Orchestrator (AI Agent):** In advanced stages, the AI Orchestrator can dynamically trigger RPA bots as part of a larger, cross-functional workflow.
## 4. Governance
RPA governance in AING™ focuses on security, resilience, and change management. All bots must operate with the principle of least privilege, accessing only the systems and data they absolutely require. A change management process is required for any updates to the applications that bots interact with to prevent widespread failures.
## 5. Tools
- **Process Discovery Tool:** An AI-powered tool that monitors user activity to automatically identify and map repetitive processes that are good candidates for RPA.
- **Bot Control Tower:** A centralized dashboard for managing and monitoring the entire fleet of active bots.
- **Reusable Component Library:** A collection of pre-built automation components that can be used to accelerate bot development.
## 6. Maturity Levels
1.  **Initial:** Ad-hoc automation of individual tasks with desktop scripts.
2.  **Managed:** A centralized team manages a small number of enterprise bots.
3.  **Automated:** RPA is integrated with AI capabilities like OCR and NLP to handle more complex tasks ("Intelligent Automation").
4.  **Orchestrated:** RPA bots are treated as a resource pool, dynamically invoked by a central AI Orchestrator to execute steps in larger business processes.
5.  **Co-Intelligent:** Bots can self-heal when they encounter errors and proactively optimize the processes they are automating.
## 7. Certification Pathway
RPA is a practical specialization within the **AING™ Practitioner Certificate (APC)**, particularly relevant for those in operations and business process management.
