# Capability Module: Autonomous Systems
## 1. Principles
Autonomous Systems within AING™, which include technologies like self-driving vehicles, drones, and smart robots, are governed by the principles of safety, reliability, and predictable behavior. The primary principle is "bounded autonomy," meaning that all autonomous agents must operate within a clearly defined set of rules, ethical boundaries, and operational constraints defined by human Guardians.
## 2. Practices
The development of Autonomous Systems in AING™ follows a rigorous, safety-critical lifecycle. This includes extensive simulation and testing in virtual environments (Initialize), managing the complex software and hardware integration (Orchestrate), developing the core AI models for perception, navigation, and decision-making (Co-Create), conducting extensive real-world testing with human oversight (Validate), and deploying over-the-air updates to improve system performance (Evolve).
## 3. Roles
- **Robotics Engineer (Creator):** Designs the hardware and software for the autonomous system.
- **AI Safety Researcher (Guardian):** A specialized Guardian role focused on identifying and mitigating potential failure modes and safety risks.
- **Simulation Engineer (Creator):** Builds and maintains the virtual "Digital Twin" environments used for testing.
- **Remote Operator (Guardian):** A human-in-the-loop who can take control of an autonomous system if it encounters a situation outside its designed parameters.
## 4. Governance
Governance for Autonomous Systems is the most rigorous in AING™. A formal "Safety Case" is required before any system can be deployed, which must prove that the risk of operation is acceptably low. All operational data is logged for incident analysis, and a clear chain of accountability is established for the system's actions.
## 5. Tools
- **Simulation Platform:** A high-fidelity virtual environment for safely testing and training autonomous systems.
- **Sensor Fusion Engine:** Software that combines data from multiple sensors (e.g., cameras, LiDAR, radar) to create a comprehensive model of the environment.
- **Ethics Engine:** A software module that allows human Guardians to define and update the ethical rules and decision-making priorities of the autonomous agent.
## 6. Maturity Levels
1.  **Initial:** Remote-controlled or teleoperated systems.
2.  **Managed:** Systems with driver-assist or operator-assist features.
3.  **Automated:** Systems that can operate autonomously in specific, constrained environments.
4.  **Orchestrated:** A fleet of autonomous systems that can coordinate their actions to achieve a common goal.
5.  **Co-Intelligent:** Autonomous systems that can safely interact with humans and other autonomous agents in complex, unpredictable environments.
## 7. Certification Pathway
This is a highly specialized module for the **AING™ Architect Certificate (AAC)**, focusing on the design of safe and reliable cyber-physical systems.
