# Capability Module: Virtual Reality (VR)
## 1. Principles
Virtual Reality (VR) within AING™ is based on the principles of immersion, presence, and safe simulation. VR is used to create fully digital environments that allow users to learn, collaborate, and experience scenarios that would be too costly, dangerous, or impossible in the real world. The key is to create a sense of "presence" where the user feels truly inside the virtual space.
## 2. Practices
Creating VR experiences involves defining the goals of the simulation or collaborative space (Initialize), building the 3D environment and interactive elements (Co-Create), using an AI Orchestrator to manage multi-user sessions and simulate complex scenarios (Orchestrate), gathering user feedback on the sense of presence and usability (Validate), and continuously expanding and refining the virtual world (Evolve).
## 3. Roles
- **VR Developer (Creator):** Builds the VR application and its interactive logic.
- **Environment Artist (Creator):** Designs and builds the immersive 3D worlds.
- **Instructional Designer (Strategist):** Designs training and educational scenarios that leverage the unique capabilities of VR.
- **AI NPC Director (AI Agent):** An AI agent that can control the behavior of non-player characters (NPCs) in a simulation to create realistic and dynamic scenarios.
## 4. Governance
VR governance focuses on user well-being, data privacy, and virtual conduct. This includes policies to prevent motion sickness and user fatigue. Data privacy is critical, as biometric data (eye tracking, movement) can be collected. A code of conduct is necessary to manage interactions in multi-user virtual spaces.
## 5. Tools
- **Game Engine:** (e.g., Unity, Unreal Engine) The primary tool for building interactive 3D VR experiences.
- **VR Headset:** (e.g., Meta Quest, Valve Index) The hardware that provides the immersive visual and audio experience.
- **Haptic Feedback Devices:** Gloves or suits that provide a sense of touch within the virtual environment.
- **Multi-user Networking Platform:** A backend service that allows multiple users to interact in the same virtual space.
## 6. Maturity Levels
1.  **Initial:** Simple 360-degree video experiences.
2.  **Managed:** Interactive, single-user training simulations.
3.  **Automated:** Multi-user collaborative spaces for meetings and design reviews.
4.  **Orchestrated:** VR simulations that are connected to real-world data from a Digital Twin, creating a hyper-realistic training environment.
5.  **Co-Intelligent:** A persistent, large-scale virtual world (a Metaverse) where AI and human users coexist and collaborate.
## 7. Certification Pathway
VR is a specialization topic for the **AING™ Practitioner (APC)** and **AING™ Architect (AAC)** certifications, particularly for those in training, design, and collaboration fields.
