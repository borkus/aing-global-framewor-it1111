# Capability Module: Augmented Reality (AR)
## 1. Principles
Augmented Reality (AR) in the AING™ framework is guided by the principles of contextual awareness, intuitive interaction, and human augmentation. AR is not about distracting users with digital noise but about overlaying the right information in the right place at the right time to enhance their perception of and interaction with the physical world.
## 2. Practices
Developing AR experiences involves identifying real-world tasks that can be enhanced with digital information (Initialize), designing the 3D assets and user interfaces (Co-Create), using an AI Orchestrator to deliver contextual data to the AR device in real-time (Orchestrate), conducting extensive user testing in real-world environments (Validate), and continuously refining the experience based on user feedback and new data (Evolve).
## 3. Roles
- **AR/VR Developer (Creator):** Builds the AR application using tools like Unity or Unreal Engine.
- **3D Artist (Creator):** Creates the 3D models and animations that are used in the AR experience.
- **UX Designer (Creator):** Specializes in designing intuitive user interfaces for hands-free, 3D environments.
- **Field Technician (Strategist/User):** A key user of enterprise AR, providing feedback on how the technology can best assist them in their work (e.g., maintenance, assembly).
## 4. Governance
AR governance in AING™ focuses on user safety, data privacy, and information accuracy. Safety guidelines are critical to ensure that digital overlays do not distract users from real-world hazards. Privacy is a concern as AR devices often have "always-on" cameras. Governance must also ensure that the information displayed by the AR system is accurate and reliable.
## 5. Tools
- **AR Development Kit (SDK):** (e.g., ARKit, ARCore) Software libraries for building AR applications for mobile devices.
- **3D Modeling Software:** (e.g., Blender, Maya) for creating the virtual objects.
- **AR Head-Mounted Display (HMD):** Devices like the HoloLens or Magic Leap for hands-free AR experiences.
- **AI-Powered Object Recognition:** An AI model that allows the AR device to recognize objects in the real world and overlay the correct contextual information.
## 6. Maturity Levels
1.  **Initial:** Simple mobile AR apps for marketing or entertainment.
2.  **Managed:** Using AR for guided instructions in a controlled environment (e.g., warehouse picking).
3.  **Automated:** AR systems that can automatically recognize objects and provide real-time data overlays.
4.  **Orchestrated:** An AR system connected to a Digital Twin, allowing users to see real-time data and predictive insights overlaid on a physical machine.
5.  **Co-Intelligent:** A collaborative AR space where multiple users, some remote, can interact with the same virtual objects overlaid on the real world.
## 7. Certification Pathway
AR is a key part of the Interaction & Immersion Layer and is a specialization topic for the **AING™ Practitioner (APC)** and **AING™ Architect (AAC)** certifications.
