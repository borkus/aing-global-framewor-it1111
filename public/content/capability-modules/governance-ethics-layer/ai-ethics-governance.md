# Capability Module: AI Ethics & Governance
## 1. Principles
AI Ethics & Governance is a foundational layer of the entire AING™ framework, not just a module. Its principles are human-centricity, fairness, transparency, and accountability. We believe that ethical considerations cannot be an afterthought; they must be woven into the fabric of the system from the very beginning. The ultimate goal is to build AI systems that are not only powerful but also trustworthy and beneficial to society.
## 2. Practices
The practice of AI Ethics involves establishing a cross-functional Ethics Review Board (Initialize), embedding "Ethical Stories" or requirements into the project backlog (Orchestrate), conducting bias audits and fairness assessments during development (Co-Create), continuously monitoring AI models for unintended consequences (Validate), and maintaining a transparent record of all AI-driven decisions (Evolve).
## 3. Roles
- **AI Ethicist (Guardian):** A specialized Guardian who helps teams navigate complex ethical dilemmas and provides guidance on responsible AI development.
- **Ethics Review Board (Guardian):** A committee of diverse stakeholders (including legal, HR, and business representatives) that provides oversight for high-risk AI projects.
- **All Creators & Strategists:** Every team member has a responsibility to consider the ethical implications of their work.
- **AI Analyst (AI Agent):** Can be configured to automatically scan for and flag potential ethical issues, such as biased language or discriminatory outcomes.
## 4. Governance
This module *is* the governance framework for all other AI capabilities. It establishes the policies, standards, and review processes that all AI projects must adhere to. It formalizes the role of the Guardian and the mechanisms for human oversight, ensuring that accountability is always clear.
## 5. Tools
- **AI Impact Assessment Template:** A structured document that guides teams in thinking through the potential societal and ethical impacts of a new AI system.
- **Bias Detection Toolkit:** A suite of statistical tools for auditing datasets and models for demographic biases.
- **Explainable AI (XAI) Libraries:** Tools that help developers understand and explain the decisions made by complex models like neural networks.
## 6. Maturity Levels
1.  **Initial:** Ad-hoc discussions about ethics; reliance on individual morals.
2.  **Managed:** A formal Code of AI Ethics is published and communicated.
3.  **Automated:** Bias detection and fairness checks are integrated into the CI/CD pipeline.
4.  **Orchestrated:** An AI-powered governance system that continuously monitors all other AI systems for ethical compliance.
5.  **Co-Intelligent:** AI agents that can reason about the ethical implications of their actions and proactively ask for human guidance in ambiguous situations.
## 7. Certification Pathway
A deep understanding of AI Ethics & Governance is required for all AING™ certifications. It is a particular focus of the **AING™ Leader Certificate (ALC)**, which emphasizes responsible AI transformation.
