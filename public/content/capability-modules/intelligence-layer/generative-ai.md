# Capability Module: Generative AI
## 1. Principles
Generative AI in AING™ is treated as a powerful creative accelerant, governed by principles of originality, attribution, and human oversight. We believe Generative AI should augment human creativity, not replace it. Every generated output must be subject to human review and refinement, and the system must be designed to prevent the creation of harmful or misleading content.
## 2. Practices
AING™ advocates for a "retrieval-augmented generation" (RAG) approach to ground generative models in factual, verifiable information (Initialize). Practices include fine-tuning models on specific organizational data (Orchestrate), integrating generative capabilities into creative workflows (Co-Create), implementing robust content filtering and fact-checking mechanisms (Validate), and continuously updating models based on human feedback and new source data (Evolve).
## 3. Roles
- **Prompt Engineer (Creator):** Specializes in designing effective prompts to elicit desired outputs from generative models.
- **Creative Professional (Creator):** Uses generative tools as a co-pilot for brainstorming, drafting, and creating content (e.g., writers, designers, developers).
- **AI Ethicist (Guardian):** A specialized Guardian role focused on the ethical risks of Generative AI, including deepfakes, misinformation, and copyright issues.
- **Domain Expert (Strategist):** Provides the curated, high-quality data used to fine-tune and ground the generative models.
## 4. Governance
Governance for Generative AI is the most stringent within the AING™ framework. A "Content Review Board" of human Guardians is required for any public-facing generative application. All generated content must be watermarked or otherwise identifiable as AI-assisted. There are strict prohibitions on generating content that is deceptive, discriminatory, or infringes on intellectual property.
## 5. Tools
- **Prompt Library:** A centralized, version-controlled repository of effective prompts for various tasks and models.
- **Generative AI Co-pilots:** AI agents integrated into IDEs, design software, and word processors to provide in-context generative assistance.
- **AI Content Detection Suite:** A set of tools used by Guardians to identify AI-generated content and check it for authenticity and accuracy.
## 6. Maturity Levels
1.  **Initial:** Using public, general-purpose generative AI tools for simple tasks.
2.  **Managed:** Fine-tuning models on proprietary data for specific styles or domains.
3.  **Automated:** Integrating generative AI APIs into internal business processes.
4.  **Orchestrated:** A multi-modal generative system that can create and combine text, images, and code in a single workflow.
5.  **Co-Intelligent:** Generative agents that can proactively identify creative opportunities and collaborate with humans on novel solutions.
## 7. Certification Pathway
Generative AI is a cutting-edge specialization available for the **AING™ Practitioner (APC)** and **AING™ Leader (ALC)** certifications, focusing on both the practical application and the strategic and ethical implications of the technology.
