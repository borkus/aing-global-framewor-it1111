# AING™ Foundation Certificate (AFC)
The AING™ Foundation Certificate (AFC) is the essential first step for any professional looking to understand the future of work in the AI era. This certification validates that you have a comprehensive understanding of the core principles, terminology, and structure of the AING™ framework.
## Why Earn the AFC?
- **Build a Solid Foundation:** Gain a robust understanding of the concepts that underpin all other AING™ modules and certifications.
- **Speak the Language of AI Transformation:** Confidently discuss the AING™ lifecycle, roles, and principles with colleagues and stakeholders.
- **Demonstrate Your Foresight:** Show current and future employers that you are proactive in understanding the next generation of management and collaboration frameworks.
- **Unlock Your AING™ Journey:** The AFC is the prerequisite for advancing to the Practitioner level and beyond.
## Target Audience
The AFC is ideal for a wide range of professionals, including:
- Team members working in or alongside AING™-driven projects.
- Project managers, product owners, and business analysts new to AI-orchestrated frameworks.
- IT professionals, developers, and designers seeking to understand how their roles evolve with AI.
- Executives and managers who need a high-level understanding of AING™ to support its adoption.
- Students and recent graduates preparing for a career in technology and management.
## Syllabus Overview
The AFC exam covers the AING-CF: Core Framework module in its entirety. Key areas include:
### 1. The Need for AING™
- Understanding the limitations of traditional frameworks in the AI era.
- The core value proposition of AING™.
### 2. The Five AING™ Principles
- Co-Intelligence
- Orchestration
- Transparency
- Learning
- Rhythm
### 3. The AING™ Lifecycle
- A detailed understanding of the five phases: Initialize, Orchestrate, Co-Create, Validate, and Evolve.
- The inputs and outputs of each phase.
### 4. Roles in AING™
- The responsibilities of the human roles: Strategist, Creator, and Guardian.
- The functions of the AI agent roles: Orchestrator, Co-pilot, and Analyst.
### 5. AING™ Tools & Artifacts
- The purpose and key components of the AING™ Canvas.
- The concept and features of the AING™ Flowboard.
- The stages of the Insight Loop.
## Exam Details
- **Format:** Multiple-choice questions.
- **Number of Questions:** 40
- **Passing Score:** 65% (26 out of 40).
- **Duration:** 60 minutes.
- **Delivery:** Online, proctored exam.
- **Prerequisites:** None, although completion of an accredited training course is recommended.
