# AING™ Practitioner Certificate (APC)
The AING™ Practitioner Certificate (APC) is designed for professionals who are actively involved in the implementation and operation of the AING™ framework. This certification moves beyond theoretical understanding to validate your ability to apply AING™ principles and practices in real-world project environments.
## Why Earn the APC?
- **Validate Practical Skills:** Demonstrate your ability to use AING™ tools, facilitate human-AI collaboration, and manage the framework's lifecycle.
- **Lead High-Performing Teams:** Gain the skills to guide a co-intelligent team, leveraging the strengths of both human experts and AI agents.
- **Drive Tangible Outcomes:** Learn how to apply AING™ to accelerate value delivery, improve quality, and foster continuous innovation.
- **Specialize Your Expertise:** The APC allows for specialization in key domains like Agile transformation (AING-AM), customer experience (AING-CX), or operations (AING-OM).
## Target Audience
The APC is the ideal certification for hands-on professionals, including:
- Project and Program Managers transitioning to AI-orchestrated environments.
- Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches evolving their skill set.
- Product Owners and Managers responsible for delivering AI-powered products.
- Senior Developers, Designers, and Analysts working in AING™ teams.
- Consultants who guide organizations in adopting new ways of working.
## Syllabus Overview
The APC exam covers the practical application of the Core Framework and knowledge of at least one specialized module.
### 1. Applying the Core Framework
- Facilitating the creation of an AING™ Canvas.
- Interpreting and interacting with the AING™ Flowboard.
- Leading "Collaboration Moments" effectively.
- Applying the Insight Loop to drive project evolution.
- Navigating the dynamic interplay between the five lifecycle phases.
### 2. Managing the Co-Intelligent Unit
- Defining and clarifying the roles of Strategist, Creator, and Guardian within a project.
- Effectively prompting and collaborating with AI Co-pilots.
- Leveraging insights from the AI Analyst for decision-making.
- Managing the human-AI interface and resolving conflicts.
### 3. Specialization Modules (Choose one or more)
Candidates will be tested on their understanding of at least one of the following modules:
- **AING-AM: Agile Matrix:** Transitioning from Scrum ceremonies, implementing Predictive Sprinting.
- **AING-CX: Customer Experience:** Applying AI to journey mapping and personalization.
- **AING-OM: Operations Management:** Implementing Intelligent Change Management and predictive monitoring.
- And other specialized modules as they become available.
## Exam Details
- **Format:** Scenario-based, multiple-choice questions.
- **Number of Questions:** 50
- **Passing Score:** 70% (35 out of 50).
- **Duration:** 90 minutes.
- **Delivery:** Online, proctored exam.
- **Prerequisites:** AING™ Foundation Certificate (AFC).
