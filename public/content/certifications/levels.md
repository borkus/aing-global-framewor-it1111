# AING™ Certification Levels
The AING™ certification program is a progressive, multi-level pathway designed to accommodate professionals at every stage of their career, from those new to AI-driven frameworks to seasoned executives leading enterprise-wide transformations. Each level builds upon the last, creating a comprehensive and deep understanding of the AING™ ecosystem.
## L1: AING™ Foundation Certificate (AFC)
**Focus:** Understanding
This is the entry point into the AING™ certification program. It is designed for individuals who need to understand the fundamental concepts, terminology, principles, and lifecycle of the AING™ framework. The AFC demonstrates that a professional has a solid grasp of the "what" and "why" behind AING™.
- **Target Audience:** Team members, managers, students, and anyone interested in learning the basics of AI-orchestrated work.
- **Prerequisites:** None.
- **Key Competencies:** Knowledge of AING™ principles, roles, lifecycle phases, and core tools like the AING™ Canvas.
## L2: AING™ Practitioner Certificate (APC)
**Focus:** Application
The Practitioner level is for professionals who will be actively involved in applying the AING™ framework within a team or project. This certification validates the ability to use AING™ tools, facilitate collaboration moments, and work effectively within a human-AI co-intelligent unit.
- **Target Audience:** Project managers, Scrum masters, product owners, developers, designers, and business analysts.
- **Prerequisites:** AFC certification.
- **Key Competencies:** Practical application of the AING™ lifecycle, use of the Flowboard, and proficiency in one or more specialized AING™ modules (e.g., AING-AM, AING-CX).
## L3: AING™ Architect Certificate (AAC)
**Focus:** Design & Integration
The Architect level is for experienced professionals responsible for designing and implementing complex, AI-driven systems based on AING™. This certification demonstrates expertise in AING™ architecture, the integration of various modules, and the technical design of intelligent delivery and operational systems.
- **Target Audience:** Solution architects, enterprise architects, senior DevOps engineers, and technical leads.
- **Prerequisites:** APC certification.
- **Key Competencies:** System design, multi-module integration, hybrid/multi-cloud orchestration (AING-ID), and AI-Ops implementation (AING-OM).
## L4: AING™ Leader Certificate (ALC)
**Focus:** Strategy & Transformation
The Leader level is designed for individuals who are responsible for driving strategic change and leading AI transformation within their organizations. This certification validates the ability to apply AING™ at a strategic level, implement intelligent governance, and foster a culture of continuous evolution.
- **Target Audience:** Directors, VPs, C-level executives, and transformation consultants.
- **Prerequisites:** APC or AAC certification.
- **Key Competencies:** Strategic alignment, AI-driven governance (AING-GM), portfolio management, and leading organizational change.
## L5: AING™ Enterprise Champion (AEC)
**Focus:** Mastery & Evangelism
This is the highest level of AING™ certification, reserved for individuals who have demonstrated deep mastery of the entire framework and have contributed to its evolution. Enterprise Champions are recognized thought leaders who can train, mentor, and guide entire organizations in their AING™ adoption journey.
- **Target Audience:** Senior consultants, internal coaches, and framework evangelists.
- **Prerequisites:** ALC certification and significant practical experience.
- **Key Competencies:** Deep expertise across all modules, training and mentorship skills, and contributions to the AING™ body of knowledge.
