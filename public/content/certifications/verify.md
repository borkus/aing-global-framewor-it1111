# Verify a Certification
The AING Global Alliance (AGA) provides a secure, real-time directory to verify the status of all AING™ certified professionals. This tool is designed for employers, clients, and colleagues to confirm the validity and level of an individual's AING™ credentials.
## How to Verify
To verify a certification, you will need the individual's full name and their unique Certification ID number.

### For Certified Professionals
Your Certification ID can be found on your official certificate and in your AING™ Member Portal. It is your responsibility to provide this information accurately to those who wish to verify your status.
### For Employers and Clients
Verifying AING™ certifications ensures that your team members and partners possess the genuine, up-to-date knowledge and skills required to lead and participate in AI-driven initiatives. All certifications are valid for two years and require continuous education credits for renewal, ensuring that certified professionals remain current with the rapidly evolving landscape of AI and the AING™ framework.
### Features of the Verification Directory:
- **Instant Confirmation:** Get immediate confirmation of a certificate's validity.
- **Level and Specializations:** View the specific certification level (AFC, APC, etc.) and any specialized modules the individual has passed.
- **Issue and Expiry Dates:** See the dates when the certification was issued and when it is due for renewal.
- **Secure and Reliable:** The directory is the single source of truth for all official AING™ credentials, preventing fraud and misrepresentation.
We are committed to maintaining the integrity and value of the AING™ certification. The public verification directory is a key part of that commitment.
