# AING™ Architect Certificate (AAC)
The AING™ Architect Certificate (AAC) is a premier certification for senior technology professionals who design and build the complex, integrated systems that power the modern enterprise. This certification validates your expertise in architecting solutions that are not just technologically sound but are also intelligently orchestrated and managed according to AING™ principles.
## Why Earn the AAC?
- **Become a Master Builder:** Demonstrate your ability to design resilient, scalable, and intelligent systems using the AING™ architectural patterns.
- **Lead Technical Strategy:** Gain the credibility to lead the technical design of AI-driven transformation initiatives.
- **Integrate Complex Ecosystems:** Master the art of orchestrating hybrid and multi-cloud environments, complex data pipelines, and integrated cyber-physical systems.
- **Future-Proof Your Skills:** Position yourself at the forefront of system design in an AI-native world.
## Target Audience
The AAC is designed for experienced technical leaders:
- Solution Architects and Enterprise Architects.
- Senior DevOps, SRE, and Cloud Engineers.
- Technical Leads and Principal Engineers.
- CTOs and VPs of Engineering in technology-driven organizations.
## Syllabus Overview
The AAC exam focuses on the design, integration, and technical governance aspects of the AING™ framework.
### 1. AING™ Architectural Principles
- Applying microservices, API-driven, and event-driven patterns within an AING™ context.
- Designing for the four AING™ Operating Layers (Strategy, Execution, Governance, Evolution).
- Architecting the AING™ Core components: AI Orchestrator, Knowledge Base, and Model Registry.
### 2. AING-ID: Intelligent Delivery
- Designing a unified control plane for hybrid and multi-cloud delivery.
- Architecting intelligent rollout strategies (canary, blue-green) managed by an AI Orchestrator.
- Designing integrated delivery pipelines for complex systems (e.g., IoT).
### 3. AING-OM: Operations Management
- Architecting an AI-Ops environment for predictive monitoring and self-healing.
- Designing an Intelligent Change Management system to replace traditional CABs.
- Implementing architectural patterns that support high-availability and operational resilience.
### 4. AING-GM: Governance & Management (Technical Perspective)
- Designing the technical infrastructure for continuous compliance.
- Architecting data pipelines to feed a real-time Portfolio Health Dashboard.
- Implementing the technical mechanisms for Dynamic Decision Gates.
## Exam Details
- **Format:** Design-based scenarios and complex multiple-choice questions.
- **Number of Questions:** 40
- **Passing Score:** 70% (28 out of 40).
- **Duration:** 120 minutes.
- **Delivery:** Online, proctored exam.
- **Prerequisites:** AING™ Practitioner Certificate (APC).
