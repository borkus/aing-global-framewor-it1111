# Exam Guide & Syllabus
Welcome to the central resource for all AING™ certification exams. This guide provides essential information on exam formats, policies, and preparation strategies to help you succeed on your certification journey.
## General Exam Information
| Feature | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Delivery Method** | All exams are delivered online through our secure, proctored platform. |
| **Question Format** | All exams consist of multiple-choice questions. Higher-level exams use complex, scenario-based questions. |
| **Language** | All exams are currently offered in English. |
| **Scheduling** | Exams can be scheduled on-demand, 24/7, subject to proctor availability. |
| **Retake Policy** | A candidate may attempt an exam up to three times in a 12-month period. A 14-day waiting period is required between attempts. |

## How to Prepare
Success in your AING™ certification exam requires a combination of study, practical understanding, and experience. We recommend the following steps:
### 1. Attend Accredited Training
While not mandatory for all levels, attending a training course from an AING™ Accredited Training Partner is the most effective way to learn the material. Our partners provide expert instruction, practical exercises, and exam preparation guidance.
> [Find a Training Partner](/academy/training-partners)
### 2. Study the AING™ Framework Guide
The official AING™ Framework Guide (v1.0) is the primary source material for all exams. A thorough understanding of the guide is essential.
> [Download the Framework Guide](/resources/framework-guide)
### 3. Review the Relevant Module Guides
For Practitioner, Architect, and Leader certifications, you will need to study the specific module guides relevant to your exam's syllabus. These are available in our resource library.
> [Visit the Resource Library](/resources)
### 4. Take a Practice Exam
We offer official practice exams that mirror the format, style, and difficulty of the real certification exam. This is a crucial step to assess your readiness and identify areas for improvement.
> [Access Practice Exams](/academy/practice-exams)
## Code of Ethics
All AING™ certified professionals are required to adhere to the AING™ Code of Professional Ethics. Violation of this code can result in the revocation of certification. By scheduling an exam, you agree to abide by these principles.
> [Read the Code of Ethics](/about/ethics)
