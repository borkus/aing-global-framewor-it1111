# AING™ Leader Certificate (ALC)
The AING™ Leader Certificate (ALC) is designed for the visionaries and strategists who guide organizations through the profound changes of the AI era. This certification validates your ability to lead AI-driven transformation, implement intelligent governance, and foster a culture where human and machine intelligence thrive together.
## Why Earn the ALC?
- **Lead with Confidence:** Gain the strategic framework and vocabulary to lead your organization's AI transformation journey.
- **Master Intelligent Governance:** Learn how to replace slow, bureaucratic oversight with fast, data-driven, and AI-augmented governance models.
- **Drive Strategic Alignment:** Demonstrate your ability to align your organization's portfolio of initiatives with its strategic goals using AING™ principles.
- **Shape the Future of Your Enterprise:** Position yourself as a key leader capable of navigating the complexities and opportunities of an AI-native business landscape.
## Target Audience
The ALC is tailored for senior leaders and strategic influencers:
- C-Level Executives (CEO, CIO, CTO, COO).
- Vice Presidents and Directors of Strategy, Transformation, and Operations.
- Heads of PMOs and Enterprise Architecture.
- Senior Management Consultants specializing in digital and AI transformation.
## Syllabus Overview
The ALC exam focuses on the strategic application of AING™, particularly in the Strategy and Governance layers.
### 1. Strategic Application of AING™
- Using the AING™ framework to formulate and execute business strategy.
- Aligning the entire organization around an outcome-oriented approach.
- Fostering a culture of Co-Intelligence and continuous evolution.
### 2. AING-GM: Governance & Management
- The principles and practice of replacing a traditional PMO with an AI-orchestrated model.
- Implementing and managing Dynamic Decision Gates for portfolio oversight.
- Leveraging the Portfolio Health Dashboard for strategic decision-making.
- Establishing a continuous compliance framework in regulated industries.
### 3. Portfolio Management in AING™
- Using AI-driven insights to optimize resource allocation across a portfolio of projects.
- Balancing innovation initiatives with operational stability using the AING™ model.
- Measuring the ROI and strategic impact of AING™ adoption.
### 4. Leading Transformation
- Strategies for overcoming organizational resistance to AI-driven change.
- The role of the Human Guardian and the importance of ethical oversight in an AI-orchestrated enterprise.
- Building and nurturing the high-trust environment necessary for successful human-AI collaboration.
## Exam Details
- **Format:** Case-study-based questions focused on strategic decision-making.
- **Number of Questions:** 40
- **Passing Score:** 75% (30 out of 40).
- **Duration:** 120 minutes.
- **Delivery:** Online, proctored exam.
- **Prerequisites:** AING™ Practitioner Certificate (APC) or AING™ Architect Certificate (AAC).
