# Support Center
Welcome to the AING™ Support Center. Find answers to frequently asked questions and get help with our certifications, membership, and resources.
## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
### Membership
- **How do I join the AING Global Alliance?**
  - You can view our membership tiers and sign up on our [Membership page](/membership).
- **How do I find my local chapter?**
  - A list of our current regional chapters is available [here](/membership/chapters).
### Certifications
- **What are the prerequisites for the APC exam?**
  - You must hold a valid AING™ Foundation Certificate (AFC) before you can take the Practitioner exam.
- **How do I verify someone's certification?**
  - Our online verification tool is coming soon. In the meantime, you can use our [contact form](/contact) for manual verification.
### Website & Resources
- **I can't find a specific whitepaper.**
  - All our publicly available documents are located in the [Resource Library](/resources). If you can't find what you're looking for, it may be a members-only resource.
## Still Need Help?
If you can't find the answer to your question in our FAQs, please use the form on our main contact page to get in touch with the appropriate team.
> [Contact Us](/contact)
We aim to respond to all inquiries within two business days.
