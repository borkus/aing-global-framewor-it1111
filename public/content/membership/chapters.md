# Regional Chapters
AING™ Regional Chapters are the local heart of our global community. Chapters are run by volunteer members and provide a hub for networking, learning, and collaboration in your city or region.
## Why Join a Local Chapter?
- **Local Networking:** Connect with other AING™ professionals in your area. Share challenges, successes, and build your professional network.
- **Exclusive Events:** Chapters host a variety of events, including guest speaker presentations, workshops, study groups for certifications, and informal social gatherings.
- **Career Opportunities:** Be the first to hear about local job openings and career opportunities from other members and partner companies.
- **Leadership Development:** Get involved in your local chapter's leadership team to develop your organizational and leadership skills.
