# Membership Tiers
The AING Global Alliance offers several tiers of membership to suit professionals at every stage of their career journey.
## Student Member
**Eligibility:** Open to individuals enrolled full-time or part-time in an accredited academic program.
**Benefits:**
- Access to core AING™ resources and publications.
- Substantial discounts on the AING™ Foundation Certificate (AFC) exam.
- Access to community forums and local chapter meetings.
- A perfect way to start your journey and connect with the industry before you graduate.
## Professional Member
**Eligibility:** Open to all professionals working in or interested in the fields of AI, technology, management, and business transformation.
**Benefits:**
- All benefits of Student membership.
- Full, unrestricted access to the entire AING™ resource library.
- Significant discounts on all levels of AING™ certification.
- Listing in the AING™ Certified Professional Directory upon certification.
- Voting rights in AING Global Alliance elections.
- The standard tier for practicing professionals.
## Fellow Member
**Eligibility:** By invitation only. This tier is for distinguished individuals who have made significant contributions to the field of AI-orchestrated work, either through practice, research, or evangelism. Candidates are typically required to hold an advanced AING™ certification (ALC or AEC) and have extensive experience.
**Benefits:**
- All benefits of Professional membership.
- Invitation to exclusive, Fellow-only roundtables and events.
- Opportunities to serve on AING™ advisory boards and steering committees.
- Recognition as a leading expert and thought leader in the global community.
- The highest level of recognition within the AING Global Alliance.
