# Corporate Training Solutions
Empower your workforce and accelerate your organization's AI transformation with AING™ Academy's corporate training solutions. We partner with enterprises of all sizes to build customized learning programs that drive measurable results and create a shared understanding of AI-orchestrated work.
## Why Partner with AING™ Academy?
- **Official Curriculum:** Your team learns directly from the source, ensuring alignment with the official AING™ framework and certification standards.
- **Customized Learning:** We tailor our programs to your organization's specific goals, industry, and maturity level.
- **Flexible Delivery:** Choose from on-site workshops, live virtual classrooms, or a self-paced e-learning model for your teams.
- **Scalable Solutions:** From training a single project team to upskilling an entire business unit, our solutions are designed to scale with your needs.
- **Measurable Impact:** Our programs are focused on building practical skills that your teams can apply immediately to improve project outcomes and drive innovation.
## Our Offerings
### Private Instructor-Led Training
- **Description:** A dedicated, private training session for your team, delivered either on-site at your location or in a live virtual classroom. Our accredited trainers will customize the content and workshops to address your specific challenges and projects.
- **Best for:** Immersive learning for project teams, leadership off-sites, and deep-skill building.
### Enterprise E-Learning License
- **Description:** Provide your entire organization with on-demand access to our complete catalogue of self-paced e-learning courses. Our platform includes progress tracking, reporting, and management tools for your L&D department.
- **Best for:** Scalable, flexible learning for large or globally distributed teams.
### Transformation Workshop
- **Description:** A strategic, multi-day workshop for your leadership team facilitated by a senior AING™ consultant. We work with you to create a tailored AING™ adoption roadmap, identify pilot projects, and establish an intelligent governance model.
- **Best for:** Kicking off an AING™ adoption initiative and ensuring executive alignment.
### Train-the-Trainer Program
- **Description:** We equip your internal trainers and coaches with the knowledge and materials to deliver official AING™ Foundation training within your organization, creating a sustainable, in-house capability.
- **Best for:** Large enterprises committed to building a long-term AING™ center of excellence.
