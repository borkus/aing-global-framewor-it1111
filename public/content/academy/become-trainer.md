# Become an Authorized AING™ Trainer
Join an elite group of professionals authorized to deliver official AING™ training and certification courses. As an Authorized AING™ Trainer (AAT), you will play a crucial role in shaping the next generation of AI-savvy leaders and practitioners around the world.
## Why Become an AAT?
- **Be a Leader:** Position yourself as a recognized expert in the leading framework for AI-orchestrated work.
- **Access Official Materials:** Gain exclusive access to the official AING™ curriculum, instructor materials, and case studies.
- **Join a Global Network:** Collaborate with a community of fellow experts and contribute to the evolution of the AING™ body of knowledge.
- **Business Opportunity:** Deliver high-demand training courses through our network of Accredited Training Partners (ATPs) or within your own organization.
## The Pathway to Becoming an AAT
Becoming an Authorized AING™ Trainer is a rigorous process designed to ensure the highest quality of instruction.
### Step 1: Achieve Expert-Level Certification
Candidates must first demonstrate their own deep understanding of the framework. The minimum requirement to be considered for the AAT program is a valid **AING™ Leader Certificate (ALC)** or **AING™ Architect Certificate (AAC)**.
### Step 2: Demonstrate Practical Experience
Candidates must provide evidence of significant, hands-on experience in applying AING™ principles or leading transformation projects. This is typically demonstrated through a portfolio of work or a detailed case study submission.
### Step 3: Complete the Train-the-Trainer Program
Successful applicants will be invited to attend our intensive Train-the-Trainer (TtT) course. This program focuses not on the AING™ framework itself, but on how to effectively teach it to others. It covers:
- Advanced instructional techniques.
- Facilitating interactive workshops and discussions.
- Effectively communicating complex concepts.
- Classroom management for both virtual and in-person settings.
### Step 4: Co-Teach and Evaluation
After completing the TtT program, candidates will co-teach a live course with a senior AING™ Master Trainer. Their performance will be evaluated based on student feedback and an assessment by the Master Trainer.
### Step 5: Certification and Onboarding
Upon successful evaluation, you will be awarded the Authorized AING™ Trainer credential. You will be onboarded into our trainer community, given access to all materials, and listed in our official trainer directory.
