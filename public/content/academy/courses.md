# Course Catalogue
Welcome to the AING™ Academy's official course catalogue. Here you will find a comprehensive list of our e-learning courses, instructor-led training, and workshops designed to build your expertise in the AING™ framework.
All our courses are developed by the creators of the AING™ framework and are aligned with the official certification syllabi.
## Featured Courses
### AING™ Foundation (AFC Prep)
- **Modality:** E-Learning (Self-Paced)
- **Duration:** 8 hours
- **Description:** Master the core concepts of the AING™ framework and prepare for the Foundation exam. This course covers the five principles, the five lifecycle phases, and the key roles and tools.
- **Ideal for:** Beginners, all team members.
### Applied AING™ for Practitioners
- **Modality:** Instructor-Led (Virtual or On-site)
- **Duration:** 3 Days
- **Description:** A hands-on course on implementing AING™ roles, tools, and lifecycle phases in your projects. Includes practical workshops and scenario-based learning.
- **Ideal for:** Project Managers, Product Owners, Agile Coaches.
### AI-Orchestrated Governance
- **Modality:** E-Learning (Self-Paced)
- **Duration:** 12 hours
- **Description:** Learn to design and implement intelligent governance models with the AING-GM module. This course is for leaders looking to replace traditional PMOs with dynamic, AI-driven oversight.
- **Ideal for:** Managers, Directors, Governance Professionals.
### Designing Intelligent Systems with AING™
- **Modality:** Instructor-Led (Virtual)
- **Duration:** 4 Days
- **Description:** An in-depth course for architects on building scalable, resilient, and intelligent systems using AING™ architectural patterns and the AING-ID and AING-OM modules.
- **Ideal for:** Architects, Senior Engineers.
