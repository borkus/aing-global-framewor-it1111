# Learning Paths
AING™ Academy Learning Paths are curated collections of courses, resources, and assessments designed to guide you on a structured journey toward a specific certification or career goal. Instead of selecting individual courses, you can enroll in a path that provides a comprehensive, step-by-step educational experience.
## Why Use Learning Paths?
- **Structured Learning:** Progress logically from foundational concepts to advanced applications.
- **Goal-Oriented:** Each path is designed to prepare you for a specific AING™ certification exam.
- **Comprehensive:** Paths include a mix of e-learning, practical exercises, and knowledge checks to ensure deep understanding.
- **Efficient:** Save time by following a proven curriculum designed by AING™ experts.
## Available Learning Paths
### Path to AING™ Practitioner (APC)
This path is designed for professionals aiming to achieve the AING™ Practitioner Certificate. It provides the practical, hands-on skills needed to apply the framework effectively.
- **Courses Included:**
  1. AING™ Foundation (AFC Prep)
  2. Applied AING™ for Practitioners
  3. A specialization module course (e.g., "AING™ for Agile Experts")
- **Final Goal:** Pass the APC Certification Exam.
- **Duration:** Approx. 40 hours of learning.
### Path to AING™ Architect (AAC)
This advanced path is for experienced technologists who want to master the design of AING™-based systems. It focuses on the technical and architectural aspects of the framework.
- **Courses Included:**
  1. (Prerequisite: APC Path)
  2. Designing Intelligent Systems with AING™
  3. Advanced Operations & Delivery Workshop
- **Final Goal:** Pass the AAC Certification Exam.
- **Duration:** Approx. 50 hours of advanced learning.
### Path to AING™ Leader (ALC)
This strategic path is for current and aspiring leaders responsible for driving AI transformation. It focuses on governance, strategy, and change management.
- **Courses Included:**
  1. (Prerequisite: APC or AAC Path)
  2. AI-Orchestrated Governance
  3. Leading AI Transformation Masterclass
- **Final Goal:** Pass the ALC Certification Exam.
- **Duration:** Approx. 35 hours of strategic learning.
> To enroll in a learning path, please visit the [AING Academy Portal](/academy).
